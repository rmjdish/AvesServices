What is OWL?

The NSHD OWL — Online Wellbeing and Lifecourse Resource — is the public discovery site for the National Survey of Health and Development, Britain's longest-running birth cohort study. It's where most people should start looking for NSHD data, before finishing the job in Condor.

The study has followed individuals born in a single week of March 1946, now into their eighties, generating one of the richest longitudinal datasets in the world — spanning physical health, cognitive development, mental wellbeing, lifestyle, and socioeconomic circumstance from birth through to older age. OWL gives researchers structured access to that metadata: search thousands of variables by keyword, browse by topic area, explore the original questionnaires used at each survey wave, and see which measures are most commonly used across research projects.

Data coverage

Condor always has the complete list of NSHD variables available. OWL is updated continuously as its metadata-cleaning project processes more of the archive, so its own coverage changes over time — for current, up-to-date figures, see OWL's own Data Coverage page rather than relying on a number quoted here.

Coverage isn't even across life stages: OWL's own data shows early-life variables (birth to age 25) are smaller in scale than later sweeps, since the study used simpler instruments in its first two decades. If an early-life variable doesn't turn up on OWL, check Condor directly before assuming it doesn't exist.

Searching on OWL

OWL offers seven ways to find variables, each suited to a different starting point:

Search Data Dictionary

Instant, filterable keyword search across every variable name, label, description, and metadata field. OWL's own recommended starting point.

Open →
Browse by Category

Navigate conceptually through themed groups — demographics, lifestyle, clinical measures, and more — without needing a variable name to start from.

Open →
View Popular Variables

See which variables get added to baskets most often across recent projects — a useful sanity check against common research practice.

Open →
Longitudinal Search

Finds variables collected with identical question text and coding across two or more sweeps, with a trajectory chart per variable.

Open →
Search by Year

Start from a point in time rather than a variable or topic — click a sweep year to filter results collected then.

Open →
UK LLC Catalogue

Searches the separate, smaller set of NSHD datasets deposited into UK LLC for linked analysis alongside other cohorts.

Open →
Global Site Search

The search bar at the top of every OWL page scans all page titles, headings, and body text sitewide — the fastest way to a known topic.
