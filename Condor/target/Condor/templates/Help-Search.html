Search help

Four ways to find data, depending on what you already know. Pick a section below to jump straight into that search.

By category

Browse the full category tree and expand or collapse branches to find the section you need. Parent categories may show zero items themselves — expand them to see the item totals for their subcategories. Currently selected categories are marked with a black bar to the left of the label. Numbers in square brackets are Showcase category labels.

Search by category →
By variable name

Use this when you already know, or can guess, the exact or partial name of a variable. This searches variable names directly rather than their descriptions or categories. Add a trailing * for a wildcard match (e.g. gluc*), use +word to require a term or -word to exclude one, and wrap a phrase in "quotes" for an exact match. Searching just * on its own returns everything you have permission to see.

Search by name →
By keyword

Search by a word or phrase that might appear in a variable's label or description, rather than its formal name. Useful when you know roughly what you're looking for but not its exact variable name.

Search by keyword →
By library

Search within a specific data library or collection, if you already know which library the variable you need belongs to.

Search by library →
Tips
  • You can combine a search with your current basket — found items can be added directly without losing your existing selections.
  • If a search returns no results, try broadening your term (fewer words, or a shorter partial match) before assuming the data doesn't exist.
Common questions
A category shows 0 items but still has an expand arrow — is that broken? +
No. Parent categories often show a total of zero themselves; expand them to see the item counts for their subcategories.
I expected to see a category or variable, but it's not there. +
Results are filtered by your account's access level automatically. If you believe you should have access to something you can't see, contact LHA using the details in the footer.
My search seems to ignore part of what I typed. +
Very short or very common words may not match — try a more specific or slightly longer term.
After you find what you need

Add results to your basket directly from the results page. From the Baskets menu you can then view, save, share, or download your current basket at any time.
