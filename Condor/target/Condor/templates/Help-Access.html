Registration & login help

Getting an account, signing in, and what to do if something goes wrong — covered here in one place since these questions come up before you're logged in to anything else.

Registering for an account

New accounts are not activated automatically. After you submit the registration form, your account is reviewed and approved by an administrator, who also sets your account type (Internal or External). You won't be able to log in until this review is complete.

Register →
Logging in

Enter your username and password, and complete the "I'm not a robot" check — login is rejected without it. If your username or password is wrong, you'll see a generic "incorrect" message that doesn't say which one — this is intentional and not a bug. You'll also be logged out automatically after one hour of inactivity.

Login →
What your account status means

If you try to log in before you've been approved, you'll see a message telling you why, based on one of three statuses:

  • Pending — still awaiting review
  • Approved — you can log in normally
  • Rejected — your application was declined
Forgotten password

If you have forgotten your username and password you can request help by sending an email to MRCLHA.SwiftInfo@ucl.ac.uk including the email address you gave when you registered, or by clicking here.

Tips
  • When emailing about a forgotten password, include the exact email address you registered with — it helps LHA find your account faster.
  • If the robot check keeps failing, it's the "I'm not a robot" step itself, not your username or password.
Common questions
I registered but still can't log in. +
Your account most likely hasn't been approved yet. Accounts require manual review before they can be used — there's no way to speed this up yourself. If it's been a while, contact LHA using the details in the footer.
I've forgotten my username or password. +
Email MRCLHA.SwiftInfo@ucl.ac.uk with the email address you registered with, and the team will help you regain access — see the Forgotten Password card above for a ready-made link.
Why was I logged out while I was still using the site? +
Sessions end automatically after one hour without activity. Save any basket you're working on before stepping away for a while.
