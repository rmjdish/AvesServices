Welcome to Condor

The NSHD metadata browser — discover and access data from Britain's longest-running birth cohort study.

About the Study & Condor

The MRC National Survey of Health and Development has followed 5,362 people born in 1946 for over 80 years — physical, cognitive, and mental health data across the whole lifecourse.

Managed by the Unit for Lifelong Health and Ageing, UCL (LHA), discovering life course influences on healthy ageing.

Condor gives researchers access to public study metadata. Full instructions on our web site.

Getting started is straightforward: search for the variables you need, build up a basket as you go, then save it when you're ready — your saved baskets stay available to load, share, or download at any time from the Baskets menu.

Condor itself gives you the metadata and the tools to build your basket — the variables, their coding, and how they relate to one another. Turning a saved basket into an actual data request is a separate step, arranged directly with LHA.

Condor Features
  • ✓ Search — by Category, Name, Keyword, or Library
  • ✓ Category browsing — navigate the full variable tree
  • ✓ Baskets — save, view, and load/share a basket
  • ✓ Variable-level metadata — full details and documentation per variable
  • ✓ Linked & longitudinal variables — track a variable across sweeps
  • ✓ Category memberships & value labels — see how variables are categorised and coded
  • ✓ Basket Management summary — after adding variables, see counts of what was added directly, added as linked, and restricted, with a filterable results table
  • ✓ Keep/remove linked variables — a reversible toggle to drop variables that were only added automatically alongside your search
  • ✓ Automatic basket splitting — a save over 500 variables is divided into several saved baskets rather than one oversized one
